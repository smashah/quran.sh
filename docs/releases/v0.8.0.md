# quran.sh v0.8.0 — an immersive reader for listening and study

This release makes `quran immersive` a more complete reading session: the ayah remains central, continuous recitation follows navigation and advances naturally, synchronized word emphasis is available with Quran Foundation credentials, and tafsir or explicitly related hadith can be opened without leaving the terminal. The established dashboard remains the default `quran` experience.

The archived v0.8.0 preview reconstructed the exact cells returned by OpenTUI's snapshot API. That capture path displaced some Quranic combining marks and made the layout look less stable than the live renderer; v0.8.1 replaces it with a real PTY recording and the responsive workspace correction.

## Highlights

- Press `p` to enter follow-play mode. Moving to another ayah immediately stops the old stream, rapid navigation is debounced so skipped ayat do not queue downloads, the next ayah alone is preloaded, and natural completion advances across ayah and surah boundaries.
- With user-supplied `QF_CLIENT_ID` and `QF_CLIENT_SECRET`, the reader requests matching audio and word timings together from Quran Foundation. The currently recited Arabic word is emphasized from verified timing segments; missing credentials or unusable timing data falls back to ordinary ayah-level playback without guessing.
- Press `w` in either reader for attributed tafsir, beginning with Ibn Kathir when the official credentialed API is available and falling back to Tafsir al-Muyassar through the open Al Quran Cloud API. Press `W` to choose and persist another available English commentary.
- Press `h` in immersive mode for non-exhaustive narrations that explicitly reference the current ayah. Quran Foundation credentials provide the bounded bilingual in-terminal reader; otherwise quran.sh opens the canonical Quran.com page instead of scraping browser-only endpoints.
- Arabic and English commentary blocks retain independent direction, long panels scroll within the terminal, and network-backed immersive features share one up-front source disclosure with `OK`, `Don't show again`, and `Cancel` choices.
- Search, study, and saved Quran position are shared across the established dashboard and immersive reader. The no-argument command still opens the familiar bookmark, cue, reflection, theme, and reading-stats dashboard.

The OpenTUI Three reader and browser design gallery remain experimental previews. They are opt-in, load their heavy dependencies only when requested, and are not required for the reading, study, hadith, or audio experience highlighted in this release.

The release passes strict TypeScript, the Bun test suite, deferred-import verification, source and standalone builds, and macOS/Linux/Windows smoke paths.
