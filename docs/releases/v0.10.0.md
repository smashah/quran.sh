# quran.sh v0.10.0 — one reader, one set of keys

This release is about clarity rather than new surfaces. A usability audit found that "mode" meant about seven different things, that study keys silently did nothing unless a particular pane had focus, that help and the command palette drew rows on top of each other at ordinary terminal sizes, and that the three readers gave the same keys different meanings. v0.10.0 fixes those without removing any feature, and moves the runtime onto OpenTUI 0.6, Effect 4 and TypeScript 7.

## What changed

- **Track reading replaces Reading/Browsing mode.** `m` turns it on or off in every reader, and the dashboard status bar always shows `Tracking: ON/OFF (m)` next to `Colours: AUTO (dark) (D)`. While it is on, every verse you move to is logged, whether you moved by keys, search, cues, go-to, the Activity panel or recitation, including the ayah you started on. The immersive and stream readers now log too, and leaving a surah shows how many of its verses you have read before asking whether to mark it complete.
- **Keys work where you are.** `b`, `m`, `c`, the cue keys and `R` work from the sidebar or the Activity panel instead of only inside a reader pane. `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` move forward and back between whole areas (Surahs, Reading stats, Reader, Activity) rather than stopping at each of the three reader panes, and the status bar names the focused area.
- **Finding a verse is direct.** `:` opens Go to, which accepts `2:255`, `18`, `kahf 10`, `yasin` or `the cow` and explains bad input such as "Al-Baqarah (2) has 286 verses". Exact `/` search results can now be chosen with `↑/↓` and opened with `Enter`, and the command palette filters as you type. Surah names are matched forgivingly everywhere, with suggestions for near misses.
- **Help you can read.** Help is grouped by task and scrolls, the palette keeps its selection in view, and both stay readable at 80×24 and 120×35. The footer legend fits the terminal width and always keeps Help first and Quit last.
- **The immersive reader stays open offline.** `Cancel` on the online-sources notice is now `Continue offline`: you keep reading the bundled text, and `w`, `W`, `h`, `i`, `g` and `p` ask before connecting. It also gains `?` help, `b`, `m`, `l` and `:` with the same meanings as the dashboard. Recite shows the next ayah as a cue and Memorise hides it until `Space` reveals it, so the four views are genuinely different.
- **Readers share where you are.** `quran immersive 2:255` and `quran stream kahf 1` open at a reference, every reader resumes the shared position, and `k` in the stream reader steps back across surahs.
- **A friendlier CLI.** `quran help` works, usage names the installed `quran` command and lists the readers, `quran read` accepts `--arabic`, `--numbers` and `--lang <code>`, `quran search` accepts `--lang`, unknown options are reported instead of ignored, and default `read` output is unchanged for scripts.
- **First run.** Arabic calibration uses the available height, marks the recommended option, explains what to look for, and accepts `Esc` for the recommended choice.

The same keys now mean the same thing in every reader. A few that people already rely on still belong to one reader and are listed in its `?` help: `1`–`9` jump to cues in the dashboard and `1`–`4` switch views in the immersive reader, `h` opens related hadith in the immersive reader, and `r`/`f` change the 3D layout and script there.

## Runtime and dependencies

- OpenTUI 0.4.5 → 0.6.6, the newest release that had two weeks of use at release time; 0.7.0 and 0.8.0 shipped days earlier and are planned for a later patch. Bun `>=1.3.14` is now required, matching OpenTUI and CI.
- Effect 3.22 → 4.0.5. Besides a smaller runtime, this fixes resource-pack errors reaching the CLI wrapped in a `FiberFailure`, which hid their readable `code: message` output.
- TypeScript 5.9 → 7.0.2, the native compiler, which typechecks this project about six times faster.
- @tilawa/core 0.1 → 0.4.0 on the existing recognition path; its new Zipformer engine needs separately licensed (NPL-1.2) assets and is not enabled.
- Held deliberately: three 0.177 (pinned by `@opentui/three`), React 19.2 (OpenTUI's reconciler), and onnxruntime-node 1.27 (1.31 enables Microsoft telemetry by default).

## Size and safety gates

The startup entry is 3,915 bytes, and `quran --help` measures 12.7 ms with 10.9 MB peak RSS on the recorded macOS arm64 baseline. The release passes 218 tests, strict TypeScript, source and standalone builds, the smoke paths, the lazy-import verifier, and every performance budget. `bun audit` reports one remaining high advisory, in zod 3 under `@opentui/three` → `jimp`; its only fix is zod 4, and jimp validates only options quran.sh passes it. The adm-zip and shell-quote advisories are patched through overrides.
